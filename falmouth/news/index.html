CIVIC

Police station: board prioritizes a new site

An Oct 2 outside legal opinion found 100 Brick Kiln Road likely protected under Article 97 (bought in 2003 partly with Land Bank funds). On Oct 9 the Select Board unanimously released it and asked staff to prioritize an alternative site; Tecton is pricing alternatives and 1–2 year delays. ~35% of the $5.15M design appropriation spent. Status as of late Oct 2026.

Source: falmouthma.gov legal opinion · The Enterprise · The Enterprise (Oct 5 mtg)

CIVIC

Sewer lift station also under review

The planned wastewater lift station on the same Brick Kiln parcel is affected; the board asked staff to keep investigating its legal status given its importance to the sewer project.

Source: The Enterprise
