Word Friends BETA

A town word game from Local Wellness Maps. Spell words on the board; Falmouth words (HARBOR, BEACH, WOODS, LIGHTHOUSE…) earn a +10 town bonus. Play pass-and-play on one phone, or invite a friend and trade turns by link.

Your move is in! Send this turn to your friend:

📨 Invite a friend (beta)

How it works: we make a game link with a code (?invite=…). Your friend taps it — no app download — and the board opens ready. After each move, the game gives you a new link to text or email back. Games save on each device. A full account/notification server (push + automatic texts) comes later.

Rules (quick)

  • First word covers the ★ center. After that, words must connect.
  • Place tiles in one row or column; every word formed must be a real word.
  • DL/TL = double/triple letter, DW/TW = double/triple word. Use all 7 tiles: +35.
  • Town words +10: HARBOR, BEACH, WOODS, HOLE, CAPE, LIGHTHOUSE, MAIN, SHINING, SEA, POND, DOGS, YOGA, SPA, TRAIL, CLAM, OYSTER, FERRY, SAIL, DUNE, MAP.
